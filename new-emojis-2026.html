When Do New Emojis Come Out?

New emojis follow an annual cycle controlled by the Unicode Consortium. Here is the typical timeline:

January – March

Unicode finalizes the new emoji candidates for the year. Emoji submitters present their proposals.

April – June

Unicode releases the official emoji list for the upcoming version. Tech companies start designing their versions.

July – September

Google releases new emojis with Android beta. Apple typically previews new emojis at WWDC or with iOS beta.

October – December

New emojis arrive on most devices. Apple releases with the major iOS update (usually September-October). Google releases with Android update. Samsung, Microsoft, and WhatsApp follow.

What New Emojis Are Expected in 2026?

The Unicode Consortium reviews dozens of proposals each year. Based on accepted proposals and trends, these are the most likely candidates for Unicode 17.0 (2026):

Highly Likely

  • Sasquatch / Bigfoot — A hairy, ape-like cryptid. Popular proposal with strong cultural presence in North American folklore.
  • Fighting Cloud / Dust Cloud — The classic cartoon fight cloud with fists and stars sticking out. Perfect for describing chaotic arguments.
  • Ballet Dancer — A dancer en pointe. Fills a gap in the dance/performance emoji set.
  • Paint Palette Knife — Completes the existing paint palette and brush art set.

Possibly Coming

  • Tug of War — Two groups pulling a rope. Represents competition, teamwork, and struggle.
  • Apple Core — An eaten-down apple core. For when you're done with healthy eating.
  • Landslide / Rockslide — A natural disaster emoji representing falling rocks and earth.
  • Orca / Killer Whale — One of the most requested animal emojis not yet in the keyboard.

Note: Emoji candidates change throughout the year. This page is updated as Unicode announces decisions. Check back for the latest confirmed list.

How to Get New Emojis First

  1. Update your OS. New emojis arrive with system updates. Check Settings → Software Update on iPhone or Android.
  2. Join beta programs. Apple Beta Software Program and Android Beta give early access to new emojis months before the public release.
  3. Update your apps. WhatsApp, Facebook Messenger, and other apps use their own emoji designs. Update apps to see their latest designs.
  4. Bookmark this page. We update as new emojis are confirmed. You will be the first in your group chat to use them.
